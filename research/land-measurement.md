# Nepal Land Measurement

## Source

The initial conversion rules are based on Nepal government-published
conversion tables from the Department of Urban Development and Building
Construction and the Department of Land Management and Archive.

## Ropani system

The hierarchical relationship is:

1 Ropani = 16 Aana
1 Aana = 4 Paisa
1 Paisa = 4 Daam

Therefore:

1 Ropani = 16 Aana
1 Ropani = 64 Paisa
1 Ropani = 256 Daam

## Area conversions

1 Ropani = 5,476 square feet
1 Aana = 342.25 square feet
1 Paisa = 85.5625 square feet
1 Daam = 21.390625 square feet

The exact values for Paisa and Daam are derived from the exact Ropani
value and the hierarchical relationships above.

## Metric conversions

1 square foot = 0.09290304 square meters

Therefore:

1 Ropani = 508.737024 square meters
1 Aana = 31.796064 square meters
1 Paisa = 7.949016 square meters
1 Daam = 1.987254 square meters

## Other conversions

1 acre = 43,560 square feet
1 hectare = 107,639.104167 square feet

Therefore:

1 Ropani ≈ 0.1257575758 acre
1 Ropani ≈ 0.0508737024 hectare

## Calculator design

The calculator should support conversion between:

- Ropani
- Aana
- Paisa
- Daam
- Square feet
- Square meters
- Acres
- Hectares

The internal calculation should use square feet as the common base unit.

Users should be able to enter an amount in any supported unit and
receive the equivalent amount in all other units.

## Important distinction

This calculator provides mathematical unit conversion only.

It must not claim that it determines the legal boundary, surveyed area,
ownership, cadastral measurement, or official land-record area of a
property.

Users should verify property measurements against their official land
documents or a qualified surveyor when accuracy is legally important.
